Tree Steps privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026 · App: Tree Steps (com.boidbit.treesteps) · Developer: boidbit

In short: Tree Steps works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never sends your steps or any other personal data to us or to anyone else.

What the app uses, and why

  • Steps from your phone's step counter (the "Physical activity" permission), to grow your tree, and to show your daily total, distance, calories, week chart, streak and history.
  • Steps from Health Connect, only if you choose it as the step source in Settings. Tree Steps only reads step counts (today and up to the last 60 days). It never writes to Health Connect and doesn't read any other kind of health data.
  • Vehicle detection. If "Ignore steps in vehicles" is on, Google Play services tells the app when the phone gets into or out of a car, bus, train or onto a bike, so that phone shake on the way doesn't count as steps. The app only receives "entered" or "left" events. It doesn't use your location.
  • Height and weight you enter in Settings, to estimate stride length (distance) and calories.
  • Settings such as your daily goal, tree design, sky and reminder time.
  • Notifications, only if you turn on the evening reminder.
  • Motion sensor (accelerometer), only if you turn on "Tilt to look around" for the wallpaper, and only while the wallpaper is on screen.

Where your data is kept

Everything above is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. Tree Steps doesn't send it over the internet and doesn't share it with us or anyone else. Vehicle detection runs inside Google Play services on your phone and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app makes a picture of your tree with your step count and opens Android's share sheet. Only the app you pick receives that picture.

Health Connect

Tree Steps' use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Step data read from Health Connect is used only to show your steps and grow your tree in the app, its widgets and its wallpaper. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never transferred to anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect's settings.

Children

Tree Steps is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. In any case, nothing leaves the phone.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, either clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Tree Steps → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app. Turning off "Count my real steps" stops step counting straight away.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
